

About Hamma Prop:
We offer a carefully designed trading program with a single account size to streamline the experience for all traders. The initial account size is ₦10,000,000, with the ability to compound up to a maximum of ₦20,000,000, representing a 2x increase from the starting account size. To participate, traders are required to pay a one-time fee of ₦180,000.

Referral program
Our referral program is built to reward both the referring affiliate and the new participant. Affiliates earn 10% commission (₦18,000) for each successful referral, while the referred user also benefits with 10% (₦18,000) off their initial fee, making this a mutually rewarding system.

Trading Models

We offer two trading models to cater to varying trading styles and risk appetites:

1. Model 1 (High Growth Potential):
   - Profit Target: 25% of the initial account size (₦2,500,000).
   - Daily Drawdown Limit: 5% of the account balance at the day start
   - Account Drawdown Limit: 10%, based on equity or balance (₦1,000,000).

2. Model 2 (Lower Risk Option):
   - Profit Target: 15% of the initial account size (₦1,500,000).
   - Daily Drawdown Limit: 3% of the account balance at the day start.
   - Account Drawdown Limit: 6%, based on equity or balance (₦600,000).

These models provide flexibility for traders who prioritize either high growth or a more conservative approach to risk management.

Profit Split Structure

The profit-sharing system incentivizes performance and long-term engagement:
- 90% of profits for the trader within the first 4 weeks of account activation.
- 60% of profits for trades between weeks 5 and 12.
- 40% of profits for trades beyond 12 weeks.

This dynamic structure encourages traders to remain active and grow their accounts over time.

Trading Rules and Policies

To ensure fairness and compliance, all traders must adhere to the following guidelines:
- Maintain a minimum of two trading days per week.
- Inactivity for more than two consecutive weeks will result in account suspension.
- Scalping is permitted, but trades must be held for at least five minutes.
- One-sided betting is strictly prohibited.
- Copy trading is not allowed under any circumstances.

The challenge account is a single-step evaluation account. This account has two models Model 1 and Model 2. Achieve the profit trading objective for your preferred model to get a Hamma Account. 

What is the daily drawdown and overall account drawdown
For Model 1
Daily drawdown: You are allowed a maximum daily drawdown of 5%.
This means that your trading account balance should not go below 5% of the day's starting equity. For example, your starting balance for the day is N10,500,000. 5% of N10,500,000 is N525,000  which means that your balance must not go below N9,975,000

Account drawdown: You are allowed a maximum overall account drawdown of 10%. That is, your balance must not go lower than N8,500,000


For Model 2
Daily drawdown: You are allowed a maximum daily drawdown of 3%.
This means that your trading account balance should not go below 3% of the day's starting equity. For example, your starting balance for the day is N10,000,000. 3% of N10,000,000 is N300,000  which means that your balance must not go below N9,700,000

Account drawdown: You are allowed a maximum overall account drawdown of 6%. That is, your balance must not go lower than N9,400,000
